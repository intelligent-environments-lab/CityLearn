Actions

An action requests an equipment operation for the next transition. Its meaning depends on the device: it may request charging power, thermal-device operation, a flexible-appliance start, or an escalator state.

Storage and EV charging actions are positive for charging and negative for discharging when supported. Flexible-appliance actions request a cycle start. HVAC and thermal-storage actions have their own conversion rules. Read Actions Reference for signs, ranges and physical units.

for names, space in zip(env.action_names, env.flat_action_space):
    print(list(zip(names, space.low, space.high)))

The action bounds describe valid requests. Device availability, SOC, efficiency and connection limits determine what is physically applied. Entity mode can report requested and applied actions separately.

Use the Flat and Entity Interfaces guide for table/map payloads, or Custom agents and rewards to implement a flat-interface controller.
