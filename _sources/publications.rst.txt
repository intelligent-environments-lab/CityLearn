=============================
Publications and applications
=============================

CityLearn is used to study building and community energy management, controller
design, demand response, comfort and resilience. The studies below are grouped
by application. Each citation opens the full entry in :doc:`references`.

Project papers
**************

* **CityLearn v1.0** introduces the control environment
  :cite:p:`10.1145/3360322.3360998`.
* **Standardizing research** describes the multi-agent demand-response benchmark
  :cite:p:`https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2012.10504`.
* **CityLearn v2** presents energy-flexible, resilient, occupant-centric and
  carbon-aware community management :cite:p:`doi:10.1080/19401493.2024.2418813`.
* **CityLearn v3** presents configurable simulation and service-aware evaluation
  for realistic renewable-energy-community control studies. Available as an
  `arXiv preprint <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21570>`_
  :cite:p:`fonseca2026citylearnv3`.

For ready-to-copy BibTeX entries, see :ref:`cite-citylearn`. Record the software
version and scenario used alongside the scholarly reference.
